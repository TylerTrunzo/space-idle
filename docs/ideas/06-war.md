# War

Builds on spec §8, which was read for this brainstorm.

## What the owner wants **[Owner]**

A check-in during a war shows:
- what changed;
- how fleets moved, both yours and the enemy's;
- any new political things to exploit;
- an overall measure of how the war is going.

## The war check-in **[Claude, owner liked]**

```
War with Vey · day 6          Standing ◀━━━━━●━━━▶  you +18 (▲ +7 since last visit)
 ⚔ Won at Orin (lost 2 escorts, they lost 5)    🏳 Siege of Tamsk: 62% → 81%
 ⛽ Their tanker convoy hit at Lyra: Tamsk siege fleet low on fuel
 🗳 Halden offers an alliance against Vey · Vey's war-weariness high: peace terms improving
 Peace now: Tamsk + 2M tribute   [See terms]
```

- **The war panel sits at the top of the news digest** (01).
- **Fleet trails on the map** replay where your fleets and the enemy's went since your last visit.
- **You see enemy fleets only within scanner range or your intel** (05). The rest show as "unknown contacts," so
  scanners, explorers and bought intel matter in war.
- **Standing is a tug-of-war meter.** It's built from planets held, fleets lost, supply and fuel, and
  war-weariness on both sides, and it shows its trend since your last visit.
- **Standing sets the peace terms on offer.** The better you're doing, the better the deal.
- **War-weariness grows on both sides,** so wars head toward peace instead of dragging on. It's also something you
  can exploit politically.
- **Political openings during war:**
  - the enemy's rivals offer alliances;
  - neutral factions pay a premium to buy from both sides;
  - the enemy's allies start to waver;
  - a tired enemy offers better terms.

## Changes to spec §8 **[Claude, owner liked]**

1. **The war toggle (03) refines "war is optional."** With it on, §8 stands as written. With it off, factions never
   declare war on you.
2. **Hardcore (03) drops two of §8's offline protections:** slower sieges while you're away, and no new wars
   against you while offline. Warnings (fleet ETAs) stay.
3. **§8's fixed ship classes (corvette through dreadnought) become hull sizes,** and warships are designs like any
   other (§13, 04).
4. **Fuel brings supply into sieges.** A besieging fleet burns fuel, so tankers and supply convoys keep a siege
   going, and raiding the enemy's tankers breaks one. Cutting supply becomes a way to win without fighting
   head-on, which suits a production-minded player.

## Unchanged from §8

Automatic battles with a report explaining the result, sieges that take time, captured buildings transferring
damaged, home worlds that can't be captured (every faction), factions never wiped out, war ending in peace terms.
