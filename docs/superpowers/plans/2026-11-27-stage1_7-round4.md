# Stage 1.7: Fixes from playtest round 4 — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix what playtest round 4 found before the app is built:
- megaproject levels stop paying back;
- the Mass Driver boosts goods that don't bind;
- the pacing bot models the wrong player;
- the away summary misleads;
- route throughput is hidden;
- contracts are never a decision.

**Architecture:** Same engine rules as stages 1.5–1.6:
- state + commands + tick, validate before mutating;
- deterministic and data-driven content;
- typed outcomes, formatted in `game_sim`.

New state fields are serialized, copied, integrity-checked and tolerated when absent. Saves are still disposable; `saveVersion` stays 1.

**Tech Stack:** Dart 3 pub workspace: `packages/game_core` (engine), `packages/game_sim` (CLI, bots, balance report), `content/*.json`.

**Spec:** `docs/playtests/2026-11-27-round4-findings.md`:
- §2: problems, ranked;
- §3: decisions E1–E9;
- §4: pacing targets.

Persona reports are in `docs/playtests/round4/`.

## Global Constraints
- `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-26-stage1_5-depth-endgame/global-constraints.md` applies unchanged. That includes no English in `game_core` outcomes, and player text built from typed outcomes in `game_sim`.
- These probes must keep passing:
  - the stage 1.5 regression probes: save/load at every check-in, the command fuzzer, catch-up vs 1 s stepping;
  - the stage 1.6 probes: report vs away, the realistic-state performance gate, the stop-level matrix, the habitat-feeding probe;
  - `balance_targets_test`.
- The fuzzer covers every new or changed command.
- `dart analyze --fatal-infos` and `dart format` stay clean.
- No suite gets slower by more than about 15 s.

## Review Focus
1. **Partial-minute steps.** An away or advance that isn't a whole number of game minutes must never report false shortages or waste. Test at 55 s, 14,335 s and 14,340 s.
2. **Megaprojects with different settings at once.** Credit weights of 0, 1 and 3, one megaproject locked, and one "near ready" while another is "always". The reserve and step-size invariance hold, and nextGoal agrees with the engine.
3. **Buy-max with a budget below the cheapest upgrade, or with huge budgets.** It buys nothing or stops cleanly, never overspends, is deterministic, and finishes in bounded time (a cap on purchases per command).
4. **Contracts sized by total storage across bodies.** One body's storage smaller than the order still lets the order be filled from several bodies, and nothing in the board display shows fractions.
5. **Grouped bottlenecks.** A group's credits lost equals the sum of its members, and the ranking stays stable between 1 s and 60 s steps.

### Task 1: Away summary honesty and round-4 bugs (E4, E9)

**Files:**
- `game_core`: flow_report, offline_summary, production (fractions over partial steps), megaprojects / projects (completion at finish time), contracts (whole-unit progress), outcome.
- `game_sim`: script_runner (headline, grouping display), docs.

**Interfaces — produces:**
- `BottleneckKind.megaprojectDiversion`, with typed fields `megaprojectId`, `resourceId`, `shareOfSales` (0–1) and `lostCreditsPerHour`.
- `BottleneckKind.keepBlocksDelivery`, with typed fields `bodyId`, `resourceId`, `megaprojectId` and `heldByKeep`.
- `Bottleneck.count` (int ≥ 1, the number of buildings grouped) and `Bottleneck.buildingIds` (list).

**Steps:**
- [ ] **False 0% inputs.**
  - Bottleneck input satisfaction over a step shorter than 60 s must use the same fraction as a full step. No false 0% appears when the store refills on the minute.
  - Tests use the casual repro shape: away 14,335 s vs 14,340 s, and advance 55 s then 5 s. The ranked bottlenecks must agree.
- [ ] **Grouping.** Group bottlenecks by (kind, body, resource), summing `lostCreditsPerHour` and recording `count` and `buildingIds`. Waste and storage-full entries compete in the same ranking.
  - Test: 17 docks short of one input give 1 row with `count` 17 and the summed loss.
  - Test: a storage-full row worth more outranks it.
  - Test: the `wastedValue` source appears in the top 3 when it is the largest.
- [ ] **Diversion.** Add `megaprojectDiversion` when auto-delivery takes ≥ 10% of what would otherwise be sold of a good.
  - Its value is the sale credits/hour forgone at current prices.
  - It ranks with the other bottlenecks.
  - Test: the optimizer's day-11 shape (a megaproject taking a top export) produces it, and the away summary names it in the top 3.
- [ ] **Keep blocking delivery.** Add `keepBlocksDelivery` when a megaproject still owes a good and auto-delivery is on, but a body's `keepAtLeast` holds the stock.
  - Test: the casual bug-3 shape (469 cores owed, 1K held under keep 1,000).
- [ ] **nextGoal choices.**
  - nextGoal skips a megaproject whose credits are still owed when it has credit auto-pay off and the player has paid nothing since its stage opened.
  - It considers the soonest milestone by current rate alongside the megaprojects.
  - Test: auto-pay off and unfunded means nextGoal is the milestone or the other megaproject.
- [ ] **Completion at finish time.** A project or stage whose `finishAt ≤ time` after a step completes within that same step, before commands are applied. Commands at exactly `finishAt` succeed.
  - Test: the optimizer's bug-1 repro, a claim done in 30 m followed by exactly 1,800 s and then a build, succeeds.
- [ ] **Whole-unit progress.** Contract and megaproject progress shown as "N of M" uses floor for delivered and ceil for owed, so it never reads "M of M" while anything is owed.
  - Test: 1 unit owed shows "of".
- [ ] **Headline float noise.** The away headline's net credits rounds to whole credits, and any |net| < 1 is shown as 0. The formatting is in `game_sim`; the typed value is unchanged.
- [ ] **Small display fixes.**
  - Depots and other non-producing buildings report `runFraction` and `effectiveFraction` as null (not 0) in `report`.
  - Buildings built in the same command batch as new habitats are filled from free crew once the habitats count, at the end of the batch.
- [ ] **Settled prices in `creditsPerSec`.** It values sales at settled prices: run the mirror long enough for prices to settle after a large upgrade spree, or estimate settled prices directly. Acceptance: on the optimizer's 26.5 h shape, within 8% of the next 2.5 h away (it was 20% over). Keep the performance gate passing.
- [ ] **Finish.** Docs updated: README, `docs/playtesting.md`, the stage 2 plan's away-screen notes. Commit per area.

### Task 2: Route throughput and contracts as decisions (E5, E6)

**Files:**
- `game_core`: logistics, flow_report, contracts, content loader (`contracts.json` schema).
- `content/contracts.json`.
- `game_sim`: script_runner.

**Interfaces — produces:**
- `RouteLimit` enum {rate, ships, destinationRoom, sourceStock, stopLevel}.
- `routeThroughput(state, content, routeId) → ({double perSec, RouteLimit binding})`.
- `BottleneckKind.routeLimited`, with typed fields `routeId`, `binding` and `lostCreditsPerHour`.

**Steps:**
- [ ] **Route throughput.** `routeThroughput` returns the minimum of:
  - the configured rate;
  - ships × per-ship capacity;
  - destination room ÷ trip time (room = cap − held − in flight);
  - source stock and production available.

  It also returns which of these binds. `state` shows `throughput` and `limitedBy` for each route.

  Tests:
  - a route with enough ships but rate-capped reports `rate`;
  - a route with too few ships reports `ships`;
  - a full destination with a long trip reports `destinationRoom`;
  - the result is the same at 1 s and 60 s steps.
- [ ] **Route-limited bottleneck.** When an input shortage on a body is fed by a route whose binding limit isn't `sourceStock`, the bottleneck becomes `routeLimited`, naming the route and the limit. The CLI says, for example: "Hull Plate into Cinder is limited by Cinder's storage (814/s); a depot raises it."
  - Test: the casual hidden-rule shape.
- [ ] **Contract sizing.** Quantity is sized so its sale value at current prices is worth `targetIncomeSeconds` of income, with `quantityIncomeSecondsMin` 600 and `quantityIncomeSecondsMax` 1800 (rolled between them).
  - It is clamped by the sum of storage caps across the faction's bodies, not the largest single cap.
  - Rules that stay: the min quantity and the gross-rate eligibility. The first contract keeps its hook.
- [ ] **Contract reward.** The reward is `rewardMultiplier` × sale value at current prices (not base price), with multiplier 3, and never below 1.5× sale value.
  - The flat income floor drops to `rewardIncomeSeconds` 300.
  - The cap stays at 1 h of income (`rewardCapIncomeSeconds` 3600), the first contract exempt.
  - The validator keeps the cap ≥ the floor and the min ≤ the max.
- [ ] **Contract tests.**
  - The optimizer's shape (24K copper ore at the floor) now asks for a quantity worth ≥ 10 minutes of income at sale value.
  - The reward sits between 1.5× sale value and the cap.
  - An order larger than one body's storage but within the total is fillable from two bodies.
  - The share of earnings stays within §4 on seeds 1–3 (bots, day 1, 7, 14). If it falls below about 5%, raise the multiplier in content and report it.
- [ ] **Finish.** Docs and content README updated. Commit per area.

### Task 3: Megaproject controls and the Mass Driver's target (E2, E3)

**Files:**
- `game_core`: megaprojects, megaproject_auto, commands (`SetMegaprojectAutoCommand` changes shape), state, save codec, offline_summary (ETAs), steady_revenue mirror.
- `content/megaprojects.json`.
- `game_sim`: script_runner, bots (adapt to the new command shape only; no retune here).

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 1's `megaprojectDiversion` (the diversion must respect the goods mode).
- Produces:
  - `SetMegaprojectAutoCommand(factionId, megaprojectId, {GoodsFeedMode goods, bool credits, int creditWeight})`;
  - `GoodsFeedMode` {off, nearReady, always};
  - `creditWeight` is an integer 0–10, default 1;
  - `nearReadyHorizonSeconds` (content, 43,200).

**Steps:**
- [ ] **Weighted credit split.**
  - Each minute, credits above the shared reserve are split among credit-fed megaprojects that still owe credits, in proportion to `creditWeight`. Weight 0 means it takes credits only when it is the only one owing.
  - Spare from one that owes less goes to the others in proportion. Remainders go in content order.
  - Tests:
    - weights 1:3 give a 1:3 split;
    - weight 0 gets nothing while another owes;
    - the reserve always holds;
    - 1 s, 60 s and catch-up are identical.
- [ ] **nearReady goods.**
  - Goods auto-delivery to a stage starts when its remaining credits are ≤ (credits above the reserve) + `nearReadyHorizonSeconds` × the credits per second it receives from auto-pay. With credit auto-pay off, the test uses credits on hand only.
  - Once started for a stage, feeding continues until that stage is paid.
  - `always` keeps today's behaviour; `off` feeds no goods.
  - The auto-sell megaproject hold applies only while that stage is being fed.
  - Tests:
    - a stage days away is not fed and its goods are sold;
    - a stage within 12 h is fed;
    - the mode survives save/load;
    - step-size invariance holds.
- [ ] **nextGoal and ETAs.** They model weights and nearReady. Test from a mixed-settings state: the ETA is within 15% of a no-command run.
- [ ] **Mass Driver bonus.** Retarget it to `productionSpeedMultiplier` for the tier 1–2 makers that bind mid-game, chosen by measurement.
  - Add an optional `appliesToMinTier` next to `appliesToMaxTier` (validated).
  - On the saved balanced day-7 and day-14 states, one more Mass Driver level must raise steady income by ≥ 3%. Report the measured gain for each candidate (tier 1–2; tier 2 only; tier 0–2) and pick the best that keeps the stage 1.6 probes passing.
  - Keep the factor at 1.15 unless the measurement says otherwise.
- [ ] **Migration and coverage.**
  - Old saves map today's `goods: true` to `always` and `credits` to weight 1.
  - The fuzzer covers the new command shape.
  - The CLI and docs are updated.
  - The bots use `nearReady` and equal weights (no retune in this task).
  - Commit per area.

### Task 4: Buy-max command, bots, megaproject value, retune, baselines (E1, E7, E8)

**Files:**
- `game_core`: commands (`BuyUpgradesCommand`), apply_* (upgrades), outcome.
- `content/`: megaprojects.json, milestones.json, buildings.json (habitat capacity cost), rules.json.
- `game_sim`: bots (casual uses buy-max), balance report and targets test.
- `docs/balance/`.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–3.
- Produces:
  - `BuyUpgradesCommand(factionId, BigNum budget, {int maxPurchases = 2000})`, returning an outcome with typed purchases (`buildingId`, `track`, `levels`, `cost`) and the total spent;
  - the casual bot's `buyMax` step.

**Steps:**
- [ ] **Buy-max command.**
  - It buys the cheapest available upgrade level, across every owned building and track, again and again until the next would exceed the remaining budget or `maxPurchases` is reached.
  - Ties go by building id, then track.
  - It never spends above the budget and never goes below 0 credits.
  - Tests:
    - a budget below the cheapest upgrade buys nothing, with a typed outcome;
    - a huge budget stops at `maxPurchases`;
    - the result is deterministic;
    - it matches a loop of single upgrade commands on the same state.
  - The fuzzer covers it.
- [ ] **Casual bot.** It uses one `BuyUpgradesCommand` per check-in with its spare credits (above the shared reserve) as the budget. Its command budget counts this as 1 command.
  - The bot test pins that the casual bot issues it.
- [ ] **Retune to findings §4, in this order of levers:**
  1. station and Mass Driver credit `costGrowth` (target: a station level costs about 1–2 days of the balanced bot's income through day 21);
  2. the station depth factor;
  3. milestones past 1T: 3T, 10T, 30T, plus station levels 12, 15, 20 and Mass Driver levels 10, 15, 20 where the baselines reach them;
  4. habitat capacity cost growth (crew stays a decision until about day 4 for both bots);
  5. contract multipliers if the share moved.

  Report every §4 target on seeds 1–3, before and after.
- [ ] **Targets test.** Update `balance_targets_test` (seed 1, sharing the existing single 21-day runs):
  - the buy-max casual: station L1 on days 3–5, ≥ 5/7, no gap > 3 days with neither megaproject levelling;
  - balanced: +20% from day 7 to 14, +10% from day 14 to 21;
  - contracts: ≤ 15% at days 1, 7, 14;
  - Mass Driver: one more level ≥ +3% on the day-14 balanced state.
- [ ] **Baselines.** Regenerate `docs/balance/stage1_7-30day-{casual,balanced,greedy,summary}.md`, keeping the stage 1.6 files. Commit per area.
