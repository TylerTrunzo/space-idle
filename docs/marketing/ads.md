# Ad copy (ready to use once the gate in strategy.md §2 is met)

Everything here describes what's in the game now. If factions ship, add them; until then, leave them out.

## Google Ads App campaign

Google mixes these pieces into ads automatically. It accepts up to 5 headlines (30 characters each) and 5
descriptions (90 characters each); check the current limits in Google Ads when you set up.

**Headlines**

| Text | Characters |
|---|---|
| An idle game that never resets | 30 |
| No ads. No purchases. | 21 |
| Build across planets | 20 |
| Mine, refine, ship, expand | 26 |
| Find the bottleneck | 19 |

Spares: "Your empire grows for good" (26), "Free idle space strategy" (24).

**Descriptions**

| Text | Characters |
|---|---|
| Build production chains across planets. Nothing you build is ever reset. | 72 |
| A free idle space game with no ads, no in-app purchases and no daily streaks. | 77 |
| Each planet has only some resources. Mine, refine and ship goods between worlds. | 80 |
| Check in for a few minutes. Your empire keeps working while you're away. | 72 |
| Station projects take days. Most times you check in, something has finished. | 76 |

**Images and video:** Google asks for images and video in landscape, square and portrait. Use real gameplay only:
- crops of the store screenshots;
- the feature graphic;
- a 15–30 second screen recording following the trailer storyboard in `screenshots.md`.

A campaign with no video gets fewer placements, so record one before the test.

## Reddit promoted post

Reddit users respond better to posts that read like a person talking than to ad copy. Turn comments **on** and
answer every question for the first few days.

**Title**

> I'm making an idle space game with no prestige reset, no ads and no purchases. It's on Android now.

**Body**

> Accrete is about production chains. Each planet has only some of the resources you need, so you mine what's
> there, refine it through several tiers and ship goods between worlds. Nothing you build is ever reset. Station
> projects take hours or days, so most check-ins have something finished waiting for you.
>
> It's free with no ads, no in-app purchases and no daily streaks. I'd like to hear where it gets slow or confusing.

**Link:** the Play listing with a tag, for example
`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=<package>&referrer=utm_source%3Dreddit%26utm_campaign%3Dtest1`, so
Play Console's acquisition report counts these installs separately.

**Targeting:** r/incremental_games, r/idlegames, r/AndroidGaming and r/AndroidGames. Exclude other games' own
subreddits.

Before running it, check the strategy's AI-disclosure decision (§6). A promoted post that people later find was
written about an AI-built game, without saying so, is the kind of thing that turns into a bad thread.
