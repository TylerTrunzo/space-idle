# Pitch

Game name: **Accrete** (chosen 2026-11-28). Studio: **Brightwork Games**.

## Taglines

1. **Build an empire that never resets.**
2. **Check in. Build out. Come back to more.**
3. **A patient empire among the stars.**
4. **Every ratio matters. Every planet is different.**
5. **No ads. No purchases. No reset. Just growth.**

Use #1 as the lead. "Never resets" is the clearest thing that sets this game apart, and genre fans notice it at once.
Use #5 in community posts, but never in the Play title (see store-listing.md).

## One-liner

> Accrete is an idle space game about building production chains across planets that each hold only some
> resources. It never resets, and there are no ads or purchases.

## Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

> Most idle games make you wipe your progress to keep growing. Accrete doesn't. You build an empire across a star
> map where every planet has different deposits, so you mine, refine and ship goods between worlds through tier
> after tier of production. The fun is in the ratios: which building to upgrade, where to put your crew, which route is
> the bottleneck. Check in for a few minutes a few times a day and there's always something worth doing, and while
> you're away the empire keeps working. There are AI factions to trade with, negotiate with, or fight, but war is
> optional, and you'll never come back to find your empire destroyed overnight. No ads, no purchases, no reset.

*(The faction and war sentences depend on stage 3+ being shipped. Cut them if v1 launches without them.)*

## About blurb (press kit, itch.io page, Reddit "about")

> Accrete is a solo-developed idle and incremental game for Android and the web. It's built for people who
> enjoy idle games in short check-ins and like production chains in strategy games. Growth is permanent: there's no
> prestige reset. Big milestones bring a victory moment and play continues. The game is free, with no ads, no in-app
> purchases and no premium currency. That isn't a hook: making money isn't a goal.

## What the playtests say people enjoy (use these as hooks)

Round 4's best moments, in the testers' own terms:

- **Coming back to finished work.** "Returning to several station and Mass Driver levels finished overnight with no
  hauling." → *"Your empire keeps building while you sleep."*
- **Upgrade sprees.** The day-1 and day-2 bursts of upgrades. → shows well in a short video.
- **Planning whole chains in the catalog.** → *"Plan a chain from ore to circuit board before you build it."*
- **New buildings reset the upgrade curve to cheap.** → *"There's always something new to grow."*

## Pitch claims that are risky today

| Claim | Status (round 4) |
|---|---|
| "Grows for weeks" | **Not yet true.** Testers would quit around days 8–12 (findings §2.1). Hold until E1 lands. |
| "Every decision matters" | Partly true. Crew stops mattering after day 2 (§2.8) and contracts are never a real decision (§2.6). Fixes E6 and E8 are planned. |
| "Factions, diplomacy, war" | Not built yet. |
