## Guardrails (from the spec's anti-goals)

- **Sell only what's in the build.** Factions, diplomacy and war are v1 goals but are not built yet (round 4 tested
  the economy only). Every listing claim is tracked in the claims check in `store-listing.md`.
- **No FOMO in the marketing either.** No countdowns, no "limited time", no streak bait. The spec rules these out
  inside the game, so the marketing shouldn't use them.
- **Don't name other games in public copy.** The genre is the reference, not any one title. Play's metadata policy also
  forbids naming other apps.
- **The honest pitch is the strong one.** No ads, no purchases, no reset, and your empire is never wrecked while you're
  away. Few games in the genre can say all of that.

## The biggest marketing risk is in the playtests

Round 4 found that both personas would quit around **days 8–12**, because megaproject levels stop paying back
(findings §2.1). If people install during a launch push and the game stalls in week 2, the reviews will say "it stalls
in week 2". **Don't push for installs until decision E1 is in and passes its stage 1.7 pacing targets.** Recruiting
closed testers (see `audience.md`) can start sooner.
