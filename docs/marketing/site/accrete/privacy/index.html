Privacy policy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Accrete, made by Brightwork Games, doesn’t collect personal information. This page explains what that means for the Android app and the web version.

What the game stores

Your save is stored on your own device: in the app’s storage on Android, and in your browser’s local storage on the web. It isn’t sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes it.

What the game doesn’t do

Accrete has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn’t include any advertising or analytics services. You don’t need to sign in or give us any details to play.

Google Play

If you install Accrete from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. If you’ve chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may give us crash reports and performance statistics that don’t identify you. We use them only to fix bugs.

The web version

The web version is served by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address in order to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. We don’t add cookies or tracking to the game.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and to add you to the test if that’s what you asked for. Our email is handled by Apple’s iCloud Mail. Ask us at any time and we’ll delete your messages.

Children

Accrete isn’t aimed at children under 13. The game collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add optional cloud saves, we’ll update this page before the change ships and put the new date at the top.
