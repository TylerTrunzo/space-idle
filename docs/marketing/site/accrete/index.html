Build an empire that never resets.

Accrete is an idle game about industry in space. Each planet has only some of the resources you need, so you mine what’s there, refine it through several tiers of production, and ship goods between worlds. The empire keeps working while you’re away.

How it plays

Growth you keep

There’s no prestige reset. Every building, route and upgrade you buy stays yours, so the empire only gets bigger.

Projects that take days

Station megaprojects take hours or days to finish each level. Most times you check in, something has completed since you last looked.

Bottlenecks to find

Every production chain has a slowest step: a refinery short of crew, a route that can’t carry enough, a dock that’s full. Finding it and fixing it is most of the game.

What’s in the game

Built and being tuned

  • Many raw resources, refined across several tiers
  • Planets that each have their own deposits
  • Cargo routes, ships and storage
  • Station megaprojects and milestones
  • Progress while you’re offline, with a summary when you return

Planned

  • Computer-run factions you can trade with, negotiate with or fight
  • Optional war: a peaceful player can still reach every planet and item
  • Attacks you can always respond to, even after a night offline
  • Research

No ads, no purchases

  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases or premium currency
  • No countdown timers or daily streaks
  • No account. Your save stays on your device

Help us test Accrete

Google Play asks new games to run a closed test with real players before release. Testers get the Android build free through Google Play and can leave at any time. We’ll ask you two things: when you stopped wanting to check in, and what confused you.
