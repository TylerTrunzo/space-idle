# Name ideas

The working title is "Space Idle". That works as a description but not as a name: it can't be searched or
trademarked, and it disappears among existing titles (Idle Space Station, Idle Space Industry, Idle Space Dynasty,
Idle Space Manager…). Keep "idle" and "space" for the subtitle, where they help search.

## What a name should signal (spec §1)

- Slow building, an empire that grows outward over weeks
- Production lines and ratios: it's a game about industry
- Growth that lasts (no prestige reset) and the game never ends
- A sense of place: a star map, not a spreadsheet

## Shortlist

| Name | Play title (≤ 30 chars) | Why it fits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starwright** ⭐ | `Starwright: Idle Space Empire` (29) | A "wright" is a builder or craftsman, so the name means someone who builds with stars. It suggests patient craft, it's unusual, and it's easy to search. | Some people will misspell it "Starwrite". Buy both domains. |
| **Accrete** | `Accrete: Idle Space Factory` (27) | Planets form by accretion: slow growth, built up layer on layer. It fits "no reset" well. | It's a real word, so search competes with dictionary results, and some players won't know it. |
| **Orbitworks** | `Orbitworks: Space Idle` (22) | Says "space industry" at once. Clear and friendly. | Generic enough that a similar company or app name may exist. |

⭐ = my recommendation. Starwright is the most distinctive and the easiest to own (domain, handle, trademark), and it
points at building rather than fighting. That matters because war is optional.

## Wider list

- **Patient Empire**: the anti-FOMO stance in two words; maybe better as a tagline
- **Lagrange**: the points where things hold steady between bodies; suits trade and logistics
- **The Long Build**: honest about the pace, but sounds like a documentary
- **Throughput**: speaks to optimizers, but too dry for most people
- **Orrery**: a clockwork model of the solar system; lovely, but hard to spell and say
- **Ever Outward**: describes the empire expanding; a bit soft
- **Steady State**: clever for "no reset", but it suggests nothing changes, which goes against "numbers getting
  bigger"
- **Starline Industries**: sounds like a company, which suits a game about running an empire's industry
- **Mass Driver**: the name of an in-game building; punchy, but sounds like an action game

**Avoid:** "Outward" (an existing RPG), and anything shaped like "Idle Space ___" (a crowded field, and it reads as a
copy).

## Before committing (the owner should check; my web searches were shallow)

1. Search Google Play and the App Store directly for the exact name and close spellings.
2. Search USPTO (TESS) and EUIPO for trademarks in class 9 (software) and class 41 (entertainment).
3. Check that the `.com` / `.gg` / `.app` domain and the Reddit, Discord, Bluesky and YouTube handles are free.
4. Say it out loud to two people who haven't seen it written down, and ask them to spell it.
5. Check it as a Play package name, e.g. `com.<you>.starwright`. The package name can never change after the first
   upload, so pick it once the name is settled.

My quick web searches (2026-11-28) found no game called Starwright, Accrete, or Steady State. They did find many
"Idle Space ___" titles. A search that finds nothing doesn't mean the name is clear, so do the steps above.
